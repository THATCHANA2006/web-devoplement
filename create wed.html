ABOUT THE PHOTOGRAPHER

Twelve years chasing light across six continents.

What began as a single trip to a national park turned into a lifelong pursuit — waiting hours in a hide for one honest frame, learning the rhythms of migration, weather and animal behaviour well enough to anticipate the moment before it happens. Every image here is unposed, unbaited, and shot in the field.
