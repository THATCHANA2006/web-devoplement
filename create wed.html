Vol. IV — Notes for the going

Go where the map runs out.

A slower kind of travel writing — routes, packing lists, and dispatches from towns too small for the guidebooks. Written on trains, mostly.

Read the dispatches Get the almanac

This almanac exists for the traveler who reads the last page of the guidebook first — the one titled "getting further out." Every route below has been walked, not researched: three days minimum, no press trips, no sponsored stays. Pick a pin, and go slowly.

What actually goes in the bag

— the almanac kit —
  • carry One bag, one pair of shoes that can walk and swim
  • write A paper notebook — phones die at the worst border
  • eat A list of three dishes to order before you research any
  • sleep One night unbooked, always, for wherever the day leads
  • say Ten words of the local language, learned properly

Why so little?

Every extra item is a decision made in advance, and travel is better with fewer of those.

The almanac's packing lists are rewritten after every trip — never planned from a desk.
